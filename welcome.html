Welcome to Daily Gratitude

You'll get one gentle reminder every hour. Let's make sure it can reach you.

Sent — look for a notification in the corner of your screen.

Chrome has notifications turned off for this extension. Go to chrome://settings/content/notifications, find Daily Gratitude under "Not allowed to send notifications," and switch it to allowed.

Also check that your computer allows Chrome to send notifications:

  1. Open System Settings → Notifications
  2. Find Google Chrome in the list
  3. Make sure notifications are turned on
  1. Open Settings → System → Notifications
  2. Find Google Chrome in the list
  3. Make sure notifications are turned on

One more thing — pin it for one-click access:

  1. Click the puzzle-piece Extensions icon at the top right of Chrome
  2. Find Daily Gratitude in the list
  3. Click the pin icon next to it

You can come back to this page anytime by clicking "test notification" in the extension popup.
