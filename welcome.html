Your computer is holding them back.

Almost always this is the operating system muting Chrome, not the extension. Follow along with the walkthrough — it loops.

  1. System Settings → Notifications
  2. Find Google Chrome in the app list

  1. Settings → System → Notifications

  3. Switch it on

Chrome is also blocking this extension.

Open chrome://settings/content/notifications, find Daily Gratitude under “Not allowed to send notifications,” and switch it to Allow.
